The story · The Leap

The Terrassic Project

A machine to look at the past.

At a Triassic fossil site, the team found amber that glows on its own: Temporal Amber. Around it, the lab built a machine to observe the past. Only to observe.

Dr. Katarina Usha went through and never came back. You step into the chamber to find her, the machine fails, and you wake up in the Cretaceous empty-handed. Follow her notes, rebuild the Leap Altar and travel ever deeper into time.

The story never moves you on its own. Eras always stay open, and players who just want dinosaurs can ignore it.

Chapters

One era at a time.

    How it plays

    Survive with what the age provides.

    Stone, bone, hide and flint. No metal, no glass: nothing crosses the Rift but living things.

    Eggs that hatch

    The female lays an egg near her. It cracks twice and hatches in a few minutes. The baby imprints on the nearest player as family and is born tamed.

    Male and female

    Sex is rolled at birth and shows on the body: males wear the display colours, females are more discreet. Crests, sails and frills change too. Only a pair can breed.

    1. Herbivores take food from your hand.
    2. Predators can only be raised from babies.
    3. Giants give in to a knockout, in v0.7.

    Taming

    Once tamed, it sits, follows and defends you, and attacks whatever you attack. Food heals it too.

    Riding and flight

    Saddle up, ride and jump with space. On the Pteranodon, jumping takes off, your gaze steers and letting go glides. Titans get a platform saddle to build on.

    You are the scientist

    Studying a species makes you stronger against it. The Field Notebook’s Guide levels up as you learn.

        Weapons of the age

          Melee damage. Spears work in hand or thrown: hold right click and let go.

          Field Journal

          Twelve objectives per chapter, in four themes. Complete two of three in a theme to earn an amber fragment. Four fragments light the Leap Altar, whenever you choose.
