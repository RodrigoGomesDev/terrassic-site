The story · The Leap

The Terrassic Project

She crossed first.

The Terrassic Project began with a discovery at a fossil site: amber that could open a window into the past. Dr. Katyusha stepped through and disappeared.

While trying to find her, you end up in the Cretaceous empty-handed. The notes she left behind are your first clues to what happened and how to find a way home.

Follow the clues or find a place to stay.

    How it plays

    Survive with what the age provides.

    Find food, build a shelter, and prepare for what lives beyond camp.

    From the egg to your first journey

    Tend to the eggs and be there when they hatch. The hatchlings will recognise you as family.

    Male and female

    Colours, crests, and markings vary between males and females. Find a pair and raise the next generation.

    1. Offer the right food.
    2. Raise companions from hatchlings.
    3. Come prepared for the tougher ones.

    Taming

    Earn an animal’s trust and bring a companion on your explorations.

    Riding and flight

    Ride across the plains or take flight on a Pteranodon. From up there, it is easier to choose your next destination.

    Field Notebook

    Dig up fossils, observe the wildlife, and record your discoveries. Each page helps you understand the creatures around you.

        Weapons of the age
