Dr. Katyusha

    Survive with what the age provides.

    Herbivores move in herds, predators follow the herds, and someone always leaves a nest poorly guarded.

    From the egg to your first journey

    Take an egg from a wild nest, set it down near your base and wait: the hatchling that comes out is already yours. The adults notice as soon as you start breaking the egg loose, so pick your moment.

    Male and female

    Colour and crest change from one to the other, so you can tell them apart from a distance. A tamed pair that is well fed will lay eggs.

    1. Food: the calmer ones take it from your hand.
    2. Egg: the hatchling is born tame.
    3. Knockout: a club or a tranquilliser dart, then food while it sleeps.

    Taming

    Your animal follows you, stays where you tell it to and joins your fights. The more you have studied the species, the sooner it accepts you.

    Riding and flight

    With a saddle, you can ride a good share of the animals. The Pteranodon flies, the Mosasaurus swims, and the Alamosaurus carries a platform you can build on.

    Field Notebook

    Dig up fossils, sketch the animals you come across and keep it all in the Field Notebook. A species you have studied well is easier to tame and to fight.

        Weapons of the age

          Knives, clubs and spears of stone, bone and flint, and a bow, a blowgun and a bola for what should not get close.

          The Terrassic Project

          She crossed first.

          The Terrassic Project began with a discovery at a fossil site: amber that could open a window into the past. Dr. Katyusha stepped through and disappeared.

          While trying to find her, you end up in the Cretaceous empty-handed. The notes she left behind are your first clues to what happened and how to find a way home.

          Follow the clues or find a place to stay.
